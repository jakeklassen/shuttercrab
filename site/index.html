Snip snip.

Fast, keyboard-first screenshots and screen recording for Windows, with colour that holds up on HDR displays.

Download for Windows View on GitHub

One shortcut

Press Ctrl+Alt+S, drag an area, let go. It's on your clipboard and saved before you've moved on.

Right on HDR

With Windows HDR on, most capture tools wash out. Shuttercrab keeps the colours you were looking at.

Records too

Ctrl+Alt+R records an area to MP4. Pause, restart or throw a take away without touching the mouse.
